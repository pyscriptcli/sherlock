# Deep Research (Recursive Exploration Protocol)

Multi-turn research protocol designed for complex investigations requiring recursive inquiry.

## Protocol Steps

1. **Broad Query Decomposition:** Break the target question into sub-inquiries.
2. **First-Order Crawl:** Scout search results and rank URLs by domain authority.
3. **Recursive Link Traversal:** Extract referenced papers, documentation links, and primary registries from the initial pages.
4. **Context Consolidation:** Merge extracted data into a unified, cross-verified evidence bank.
5. **Conflict Resolution:** When two sources provide conflicting dates or numbers, prioritize official documentation over media secondary reporting.
